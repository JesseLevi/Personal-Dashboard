## Anatomy
- Header: `PageHeader` with the period as title (never wraps). The title is a button (`PageHeaderTitle` `trigger`, a chevron after the text) that opens `AgendaDatePicker`: a portalled `Dropdown` popover (`role="dialog"`) with an `InputField type="date"` and a **Ga naar datum** button; Enter or the button jumps, Escape and an outside press close it and Escape returns focus to the title. It confirms explicitly because a native date field fires `change` for every complete value while typing, so a year typed digit by digit would navigate. `Week 42` (`text-text-secondary`, ISO week of the first shown day) sits beside the title in the week, three-day and list views. From `lg`, right-aligned: a `ButtonToggle size="small"` for Dag/Week/Maand/Lijst (`w-96`, 40 px high like the buttons), then previous / Vandaag / next (Filled), **Plannen** (Filled, `PanelRight`, `aria-pressed` follows the tray), **⋯** (`AgendaMoreMenu`) and **+ Nieuw** (`variant="white"`, so it differs from the Filled buttons). The group wraps under the toggle when the page is too narrow. Below `lg` (tablet portrait counts as mobile): one row with the title, previous, next and ⋯; the toggle (icons only) on its own row; Vandaag and the tray toggle move into the ⋯ menu; + Nieuw becomes a square floating White icon button (`size-14`, `rounded-control`, `shadow-sm`) at the bottom right, above the safe area. Both + Nieuw buttons open the event panel in create mode for the next full hour, one hour long (`nextFullHourSpan`, Agenda zone), until quick add (ADR-0041) replaces that.
- Body: `AgendaBody` gives the calendar a definite height (`100dvh` minus the chrome, at least 28rem) and `pr-page-right-equal`; the grid scrolls inside. The calendar fills the width. The `PlanDrawer` is a mode: closed by default, opened by Plannen, remembered in cookie `agenda-tray` (`open` / `closed`, read in `page.tsx` like `agenda-view`, so the first render is right). Open, it sits beside the calendar from `lg` once the page container is at least 44rem wide (container query; the sidebar and the 128px page padding leave a 1024px viewport only about 570px), under it otherwise (opening it then scrolls it into view). Closed, it stays mounted but `hidden`, so it keeps its tab and search.
- Event panel: one persistent `SidePanel` for create (tap or drag an empty slot) and edit; an imported event opens it read-only (disabled fields, no footer, "Uit <agendanaam>" above them). A saved event, imported or own, also shows "Gekoppeld" with its recipes, cards and notes (`AgendaEventLinks.md`). Delete is the two-step `DeleteConfirm`.
- Repeat (ADR-0042): an own event has **Herhalen** (`Niet`, `Elke dag`, `Elke week`, `Om de week`, `Elke maand`, `Elk jaar`) and, when it repeats, **Eindigt** (`Nooit` / `Op datum` with a date field), below the end date. An imported event shows neither. Saving or deleting an occurrence of a series (`item.recurring`) swaps the footer for the scope choice, in the stacked `DeleteConfirm` form: a prompt, then `Alleen deze` / `Deze en volgende` / `Alle` and `Annuleren`. `Alleen deze` is left out of the save choice when the rule was changed, since it leaves the rule alone. An occurrence has no `Niet`; a stored rule none of the choices makes shows as `Aangepast`.
- Meal panel (`AgendaMealPanel`, `SidePanel`): recipe (a `Button` that opens the recipe page), moment (select), servings (`PortionStepper`), date (disabled while the meal is attached to an event), "Naar mand" (`AnimatedAddToBasketButton` with the #429 basket popover), Verwijderen (`DeleteConfirm`) and Opslaan. "Naar mand" saves pending edits first so the basket gets the servings on screen.
- Above the calendar: basket notices after a sync deleted an event with a meal (`AgendaSyncNotices`). `page.tsx` also starts the lazy sync in `after()`.
- Clicks: own event opens the event panel; meal opens the meal panel; deadline and planned card open the card in Te doen (`/te-doen?card=<id>`).

## Keyboard shortcuts
`useAgendaShortcuts` (`document` `keydown`) maps `lib/agenda/shortcuts.ts`: `T` today, `D`/`W`/`M`/`L` view, `←`/`→` previous/next, `P` plan tray (`N` is reserved for quick add, ADR-0041). It does nothing while typing (input, textarea, select, contenteditable), with Ctrl, Cmd, Alt or Shift held, for an already handled key (`defaultPrevented`, so the view toggle's own arrow keys win), or while a panel or popover is open: `shortcutsBlocked` is the event panel, meal panel, calendars panel or a header popover (`onOverlayChange` from the header: the ⋯ menu and the date picker). The ⋯ menu's **Sneltoetsen** item (desktop only) shows the list as a menu step. `W` below `lg` stores `week`, which shows as three days. The view toggle follows `view` (`ButtonToggle` `value`), so a key press moves it.

## Deliberate decisions
- **Time zone**: one fixed zone, `Europe/Amsterdam` (`AGENDA_TIME_ZONE`), until Atlas stores one per user. The calendar runs in the browser zone and shows wall-clock strings; `spanToUtc` reads them in the Agenda zone. The now line and "today" in the grid follow the browser zone.
- **View per device**: cookie `agenda-view`, so the first paint has the right view. `threeDay` is never stored.
- **Data**: the server renders the month of today plus a week each side (`loadWindow`); leaving it calls `loadAgendaRange` and keeps the old items until the new ones arrive. After a save, delete or plan the window reloads.
- **Drag and resize save at once**: items update optimistically; a refusal calls `revert` and restores the items.
- **Planned cards**: `cards.planned_for` is a date. Dropping a card from the Kaarten tab, picking a day in its plan menu, or dragging a planned item to another day calls `planCard({ cardId, plannedFor })` (`te-doen/actions.ts`; `plannedFor` is required, `null` clears). A planned item always stays an all-day item: dropped on an hour slot it still takes only the date. Dragging it onto the plan drawer (`onItemDropOut`) sets `null`. Access is the cards policies alone, no app-side ownership check (ADR-0035): a refused write reads `read-only`, a card RLS hides reads `not-found`. After a save the drawer's copy of the card and the window reload. Tapping a planned item opens the card in Te doen.
- **Meals are day items**: always all-day, on their date, grouped per moment (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) and ordered by `position` in the all-day zone (`compareEventOrder`), never a block on the hour grid. A meal attached to an event takes that event's date, not its time.
- **Planning**: dropping a recipe on a day makes a meal plan (`planned_on`, moment `dinner`, the recipe's servings, last in its group). Dropping it on an event (found by `data-agenda-event-id` under the pointer) attaches it (`calendar_event_id`), so it follows the event. Dragging a meal to another day sets `planned_on` and detaches it. The plan buttons (drawer row, recipe page `...` menu) do the same with a chosen day and moment.
- **Week naar mand** lives in the ⋯ menu (a basket choice replaces the items when there is more than one basket; success shows a `Toast`) and as the footer of the Recepten tab (`PlanRecipeList`, with a one-line explanation). Both call the same `onSend`. It sends the visible week (Monday to Monday, from the shown date, also in month view); meals without a basket item (`shopping_list_items.meal_plan_id`) are scaled to their own servings and the same ingredient and unit are summed. A merged line carries the first contributing meal's id (see `docs/open-questions.md#agenda-week-basket-merged-line-attribution`). Nothing to send is an error message (`nothing-to-send`), and the button shows success only after a successful result.
- **Own events only in a manual calendar, synced ones read-only**: `actions.ts` checks both (`read-only`) on top of RLS and maps `42501` to the same code. Errors are codes, never database text (`agenda.errors.*`).
- **Recurring own events are materialised** (ADR-0042, migration `20261018000003`): a `calendar_event_series` row holds the rule, each occurrence is a `calendar_events` row with `series_id`, `occurrence_on` and `series_detached`. The writes are `security invoker` RPCs (`create_event_series`, `update_event_series`, `delete_event_series`, `extend_event_series`); `actions.ts` picks the RPC from `series_id` and maps `AG403/AG404/AG422` to `read-only`, `not-found`, `invalid-input`. `page.tsx` calls `extend_event_series` in `after()` next to the lazy sync, so a series keeps 400 days ahead. Scopes: `this` detaches the row; `following` ends the series the day before and moves this and the later (not detached) rows to a new series, keeping their ids, or changes the series in place on its first occurrence; `all` changes the series and the rows from today on that are not detached, so earlier ones stay history. Rows are paired with the new dates in date order, so a changed rule drops or adds rows at the end. Delete follows the same scopes; one deleted occurrence goes into `excluded_on`. Drag and resize call `moveEvent`, which sets `series_detached`. A dragged occurrence keeps its `occurrence_on`.
- **The timeline's repeat flag** comes from `series_id` in `agenda_items()` (dropped and recreated with the extra column), not a second query: a month of daily series would put hundreds of ids in a `.in()` filter. `AgendaEventItem.recurring` is set only when `series_id` is set, and `getAgendaEvent` returns the series rule (`repeat`) for the panel.
- All-day events are stored as UTC midnights with an exclusive end; the panel shows the last day inclusive.
- `position` is not in the timeline function, so `getAgendaItems` reads it for the meals in one extra query.

## Token gaps and flags
- `h-[calc(100dvh-…)]`, `min-h-[28rem]`, `lg:@min-[44rem]:w-80` and `h-[32rem]` in `AgendaBody` are layout sizes, not scale values.
- No token for a disabled field: read-only inputs look editable; a notice says why.
- Icons borrowed from the closed set: view toggle `Calendar`, `Columns3`, `Table`, `List`; the recipe link uses `CookingPot`; the Sneltoetsen menu item uses `Lightbulb` (no keyboard icon).

## Gotchas
- `PageHeaderTitle` morphs the title on every previous/next press.
- The ⋯ menu is portalled (`usePopoverPosition`, `strategy: "fixed"`) with `role="menu"`, arrow keys, Escape and focus return; items hidden by a breakpoint are skipped when focusing.
- The floating + Nieuw is `fixed`: it only works while no ancestor keeps a `filter` (the page-transition blur is dropped after it plays, see `AppShellNav`).
- The mobile header and bottom-sheet shapes follow the viewport, not the container: Ladle stories only baseline the desktop shape. The "Week" button below `lg` hugs its visible face (the hidden face leaves the flow), so it grows when "Toegevoegd" shows. The week turns into three days below `lg` (`NARROW_QUERY` in `AgendaCalendar`, 1023px).
- `page.tsx` cannot be tried against hosted until the agenda migrations are applied there, `20261018000003` (series, `agenda_items()` with `series_id`) included: without it the timeline function has the old shape and the repeat flag, the panel's rule and the RPCs fail.
- Occurrences are generated in the series' wall-clock zone (`Europe/Amsterdam`), so 09:00 stays 09:00 over the 25 October change. A month or year without the date (31 January, 29 February) is skipped, as in RFC 5545. A series that would hold over 2000 rows is refused (`invalid-input`).
- `getAgendaEvent` and `getMealPlan` are separate reads because `agenda_items` carries no location, description or recipe id.
- A meal whose ingredients were all merged into earlier meals' lines has no basket item of its own, so a second "Week naar mand" would send it again.
