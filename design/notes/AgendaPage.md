## Keyboard shortcuts
`useAgendaShortcuts` (`document` `keydown`) maps `lib/agenda/shortcuts.ts`: `T` today, `D`/`W`/`M`/`L` view, `←`/`→` previous/next, `N` quick add (ADR-0041). It does nothing while typing (input, textarea, select, contenteditable), with Ctrl, Cmd, Alt or Shift held, for an already handled key (`defaultPrevented`, so the view toggle's own arrow keys win), or while a panel or popover is open: `shortcutsBlocked` is the event panel, meal panel, calendars panel or a header popover (`onOverlayChange` from the header: the ⋯ menu and the date picker). The ⋯ menu's **Sneltoetsen** item (desktop only) shows the list as a menu step. Below `lg` the view keys still store a view, but the page shows one day. The view toggle follows `view` (`ButtonToggle` `value`), so a key press moves it.

## Deliberate decisions
- **Time zone**: one fixed zone, `Europe/Amsterdam` (`AGENDA_TIME_ZONE`), until Atlas stores one per user. The calendar runs in the browser zone and shows wall-clock strings; `spanToUtc` reads them in the Agenda zone. The now line and "today" in the grid follow the browser zone.
- **View per device**: cookie `agenda-view`, so the first paint has the right view. `threeDay` is never stored.
- **Colour and visibility** (ADR-0040): `getAgendaItems` reads `calendars.color` in one extra query and `lib/agenda/map.ts` puts it on each event (`calendarId` → `color`; unknown id → blue). `page.tsx` also reads cookie `agenda-hidden` (`lib/agenda/hidden.ts`: calendar ids and the kinds `meal`, `planned_card`, `deadline`, dot-separated) and the calendar overview (`getCalendarOverview`); `AgendaPage` filters the loaded items by that set before the calendar gets them, so hidden items stay loaded and reappear at once. A hidden category still takes new events; they just do not show until it is shown again.
- **Changing an event's category** is `updateEvent` with `calendarId`: the fields are written first (with the series scope), then `move_event_calendar` moves the event. On a series occurrence the scope applies: `this` takes the occurrence out of its series (its date joins `excluded_on`) as a single event, `following` moves it and the later occurrences into a new series of the new calendar, `all` moves the whole series. Attached meal plans follow their event.
- **Data**: the server renders the month of today plus a week each side (`loadWindow`); leaving it calls `loadAgendaRange` and keeps the old items until the new ones arrive. After a save, delete or plan the window reloads.
- **Drag and resize save at once**: items update optimistically; a refusal calls `revert` and restores the items.
- **Planned cards**: `cards.planned_for` is a date. Planning a card in quick add, picking a day in the card panel, or dragging a planned item to another day calls `planCard({ cardId, plannedFor })` (`te-doen/actions.ts`; `plannedFor` is required, `null` clears). A planned item always stays an all-day item: dropped on an hour slot it still takes only the date. Access is the cards policies alone, no app-side ownership check (ADR-0035): a refused write reads `read-only`, a card RLS hides reads `not-found`. After a save quick add's copy of the card and the window reload. Tapping a planned item opens the card in Te doen.
- **Meals are day items**: always all-day, on their date, grouped per moment (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) and ordered by `position` in the all-day zone (`compareEventOrder`), never a block on the hour grid. A meal attached to an event takes that event's date, not its time.
- **Planning** goes through quick add (ADR-0041, ADR-0058): Maaltijd searches the recipes and makes a meal plan (`planned_on`, the chosen moment, the recipe's servings, last in its group); Te doen takes "Nieuwe kaart" or an open card. Dragging a meal to another day sets `planned_on` and detaches it. The recipe page's `...` menu plans with a chosen day and moment. A meal attached to an event (`calendar_event_id`) keeps following it, but nothing in the UI attaches a new one. Planning a recipe settles its open `cook_rhythm` signal (`createMealPlan`); that signal shows on the day cards (ADR-0059).
- **Week naar mand** lives in the ⋯ menu (a basket choice replaces the items when there is more than one basket; success shows a `Toast`) (`onSend`). It sends the visible week (Monday to Monday, from the shown date, also in month view); meals without a basket item (`shopping_list_items.meal_plan_id`) are scaled to their own servings and the same ingredient and unit are summed. A merged line carries the first contributing meal's id (see `docs/open-questions.md#agenda-week-basket-merged-line-attribution`). Nothing to send is an error message (`nothing-to-send`), and the button shows success only after a successful result.
- **Own events only in a manual calendar, synced ones read-only**: `actions.ts` checks both (`read-only`) on top of RLS and maps `42501` to the same code. Errors are codes, never database text (`agenda.errors.*`).
- **Recurring own events are materialised** (ADR-0042, migration `20261018000003`): a `calendar_event_series` row holds the rule, each occurrence is a `calendar_events` row with `series_id`, `occurrence_on` and `series_detached`. The writes are `security invoker` RPCs (`create_event_series`, `update_event_series`, `delete_event_series`, `extend_event_series`); `actions.ts` picks the RPC from `series_id` and maps `AG403/AG404/AG422` to `read-only`, `not-found`, `invalid-input`. `page.tsx` calls `extend_event_series` in `after()` next to the lazy sync, so a series keeps 400 days ahead. Scopes: `this` detaches the row; `following` ends the series the day before and moves this and the later (not detached) rows to a new series, keeping their ids, or changes the series in place on its first occurrence; `all` changes the series and the rows from today on that are not detached, so earlier ones stay history. Rows are paired with the new dates in date order, so a changed rule drops or adds rows at the end. Delete follows the same scopes; one deleted occurrence goes into `excluded_on`. Drag and resize call `moveEvent`, which sets `series_detached`. A dragged occurrence keeps its `occurrence_on`.
- **The timeline's repeat flag** comes from `series_id` in `agenda_items()` (dropped and recreated with the extra column), not a second query: a month of daily series would put hundreds of ids in a `.in()` filter. `AgendaEventItem.recurring` is set only when `series_id` is set, and `getAgendaEvent` returns the series rule (`repeat`) for the panel.
- All-day events are stored as UTC midnights with an exclusive end; the panel shows the last day inclusive.
- `position` is not in the timeline function, so `getAgendaItems` reads it for the meals in one extra query.

## Mobile: date opener and pull-up sheet (#733, ADR-0059)
Below `lg`: sticky header, the day cards scrolling under it, and a fixed sheet holding the week strip and the day grid. One DOM for both breakpoints, switched with `max-lg:`/`lg:`, so nothing flashes at hydration.
- **Header**: the title is the full weekday ("Donderdag", still the date-picker button) with `PageHeader`'s `subtitle` (the date, `text-base text-text-secondary`) under it; a `size-2` dot in `bg-event-red-text` (`aria-hidden`, the colour of today in `AgendaDayStrip`) follows only on today. ⋯ and + Nieuw stay right, top-aligned; below 360px "Nieuw" is `sr-only` (icon only, like the tab bar's labels) so 320px fits. The strip left the header. The header's `EnterFromLogin` wrapper is `max-lg:sticky` (the wrapper is what must stick: `PageHeader`'s own `sticky` has no room inside a wrapper as tall as itself).
- **Sheet** (`AgendaDaySheet`, a `section` `aria-label` "Agenda", no scrim, no focus trap, `z-20`: over the page, under the tab bar `z-30`, scrim `z-40`, panels and quick add `z-50`): `fixed`, bottom edge on the tab bar (`3.5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; from `md` there is no tab bar, so `bottom-0` and `left-[198px]` clear the expanded sidebar), top edge on the header's bottom. The top is measured, not assumed: a `ResizeObserver` on the header plus scroll and resize write `--agenda-sheet-top` on the sheet (the header's bottom moves for the first pixels of scroll); before the measurement it falls back to safe area + 5.5rem. Surface as `SidePanel`'s mobile sheet: `bg-surface-highlight-white`, `rounded-tl-2xl rounded-tr-control-outer`, `border-t border-stroke-primary`, the same grabber (`h-1 w-12 rounded-full bg-stroke-primary`). The calendar's own frame is flattened inside it (`border-t border-stroke-lines` instead).
- **Stops**: *full* is `translateY(0)`, *peek* `translateY(calc(100% - var(--agenda-peek)))`. The height never changes between stops, only `transform`, so FullCalendar never re-measures on a stop change. `transform` over `--duration-base` with `--sheet-ease`; none with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Every landing starts in peek; nothing is stored; picking another day keeps the stop. State lives in `AgendaPage` (`AgendaBody` takes `sheet`: `stop`, `onStopChange`, `strip`).
- **Peek** (`--agenda-peek` 11rem on `AgendaBody`): grabber zone (2rem) + strip (about 6rem) + about 3.5rem of grid, which is `inert`, faded to the surface over its last 3rem (`bg-gradient-to-b` to `surface-highlight-white`) and covered by a transparent tap button (`aria-hidden`, `tabIndex -1`): a tap opens the sheet.
- **Full**: the grid scrolls itself (`overscroll-behavior: contain` from the calendar), the day swipe (`useAgendaSwipe`) works as before, the cards region is `inert`.
- **Controls**: the grabber is a `button` (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` the grid, "Agenda uitklappen"/"Agenda inklappen"); a tap toggles. Escape with focus in the sheet, in full, goes to peek and focuses the grabber. "+N meer" in the timeline card (`onShowMoreEvents`) opens the sheet instead of expanding in place; from `lg` it still expands in place.
- **Drag** (`use-sheet-snap.ts`, a sibling of `use-sheet-drag.ts` with its constants; imperative writes, no state per pointermove): grabber (`touch-none`) and strip (`touch-pan-x`) are the drag zone, so the browser owns a sideways strip scroll (it ends the gesture with `pointercancel`, which returns to the start) and only a vertical drag moves the sheet. After `DRAG_SLOP_PX` the direction is fixed. The grid never drags the sheet. Release (`settleAgendaSheet`, `lib/agenda/sheet.ts`, tested in `tests/agenda-sheet.test.ts`): a flick at `DRAG_CLOSE_VELOCITY_PX_MS` and at least 20px goes the way of the finger; against the pull it returns to the start; else the nearest stop. `onSettle` runs in `flushSync` before the inline `transform` is dropped, so the class change animates from the finger.
- **Cards region**: bottom padding `--agenda-peek`, so the last card clears the sheet (at 320x568 the timeline card shows in full).
- **Skeleton**: `AgendaPageSkeleton` (and `loading.tsx`) show the same header (today's weekday and date below `lg`, with the dot), card placeholders and the sheet in peek.

## Day swipe (#666, #713)
Below `lg` the day follows the finger, like Apple Calendar. `AgendaBody` takes `swipe` (the shown `date`, `onStep`, `renderNeighbour`, optional `onPeek`) only below `lg`; from `lg` it is a plain frame.
- **Panes**: the viewport (`overflow-x-clip`, `touch-pan-y touch-pinch-zoom`) holds one pane that carries the calendar. The neighbour day is a second, read-only `AgendaCalendar` (`entrance={false}`, `inert`, `aria-hidden`, `data-agenda-peek`) placed in the same pane one width plus the gap away (Gap and no selecting), so one `translate3d` on the pane moves both. `touch-pan-y` makes the browser own only vertical panning (it locks to the first direction itself, also on iOS) and keeps pinch-zoom.
- **Gesture** (`createAgendaSwipe`, imperative writes on the pane, no React state per touchmove; the shape of `lib/dom/use-sheet-drag.ts`): the axis locks once, after 10px, to x when the stroke is wider than tall; a vertical stroke stays a normal scroll and never turns into a drag mid-gesture. It never starts from the outer 24px (the browser's back gesture), with two fingers, or while FullCalendar shows a drag mirror or selection. The lock must happen within `AGENDA_LONG_PRESS_MS` (350ms) of the touch: a finger that rests that long belongs to FullCalendar's long-press event drag or selection, which therefore keeps working untouched (it needs no horizontal travel in one day; a sideways move during it does nothing). The pane follows one-to-one from the touch-down point, clamped to one pane width plus the gap.
- **Gap and no selecting** (#734): the neighbour sits one pane width plus `inline-lg` (16px) away (`left-[calc(100%+var(--spacing-inline-lg))]`); the controller reads the gap once at the lock (a probe element of `w-inline-lg`) and settles to `±(width + gap)`; the commit threshold stays a third of the pane width. A swipe never selects: the finger is still over the slot it started on, which FullCalendar reads as a tap (`dateClick`) or a long-press selection. From the lock until the next touchstart (and on destroy) the controller sets `data-agenda-swiping` on the viewport, `AgendaCalendar`'s `select` unselects and returns under it, and `globals.css` hides `.fc-highlight` and `.fc-event-mirror` there. The reduced-motion step-on-release sets it too.
- **Snap** (`lib/agenda/swipe.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/agenda-swipe.test.ts`): on release it settles to the neighbour when the pull is past a third of the pane width, or when the velocity over the last 100ms is at least 0.5 px/ms (the sheet drag's number) and the pull is at least 20px; a fast flick back against the pull returns. Otherwise it eases back. The snap runs `transform` over `--duration-base` with `--ease-base` (no new token). The date changes once: `onStep` fires after the snap ends, the pane stays on the neighbour until FullCalendar itself shows the new date, then the transform resets in one step, so nothing flashes. Taps in the strip and the desktop arrows stay instant.
- **Scroll position**: the neighbour opens at the shown day's scrollTop, and below `lg` the day view sets `scrollTimeReset: false`, so the time of day stays across days (a plain date change used to jump back to the start time).
- **Reduced motion**: nothing follows the finger and no neighbour is drawn; the old step-on-release remains (`swipeStep`: at least 50px, twice as wide as tall, within 600ms).
- **Neighbour approach** (the trade-off): FullCalendar instances are heavy (a first mount measured about 30ms on a fast desktop and about 110-190ms under 4x CPU throttling in Chromium). Three permanent panes would triple every item update and clock tick on a phone for a gesture used now and then; a hand-drawn light neighbour would duplicate FullCalendar's overlap layout and item content. So there is one extra instance, mounted when the axis locks (the direction is known then) and kept 4s after the gesture, because swipes come in runs: the next drag only retargets it (no long task under 4x throttling). It shares `visibleItems`, so the data is the loaded window; a neighbour outside the window starts `loadAround` through `onPeek` (the window always holds the shown day, so the shown items stay). The cost left is one hitch at the first drag after a pause on a slow phone, and a double `gotoDate` at each commit. The `Swipe` story in `AgendaPageLayout.stories.tsx` wires it (touch emulation at 390px); it has no baseline-worthy interaction, only the resting state.

## Quick-add sheet animation
- Open: the sheet slides `translateY(100%)` -> 10px above rest at 70% -> 0 (a bounce, `--sheet-bounce` 10px: a fixed overshoot in pixels, since an easing overshoot would scale with the sheet height) over `--sheet-open-duration` 420ms, the scrim fades in alongside. A `box-shadow` inside the `sheet-in` keyframes (colour `--sheet-fill`: `surface-primary`) fills the gap under the sheet during the overshoot. Close (X, scrim, Escape, add, Meer details, drag): slides down over `--sheet-close-duration` 220ms, scrim fades out, then it unmounts. Curve `--sheet-ease` = `--blur-fade-ease`. Variables sit next to the blur-fade ones in `app/globals.css`; utilities `animate-sheet-in/out`, `animate-scrim-in/out`. `animate-sheet-in` fills `backwards` only, so the inline drag transform is not overridden once it ends. `prefers-reduced-motion`: no slide or bounce, a short opacity fade. `SidePanel` below `md` shares the open keyframe.
- Drag: the grabber strip (`touch-none`) is the handle, via the shared `lib/dom/use-sheet-drag.ts` (slop 8px, close at 120px or 0.5 px/ms, only downward, never from the X button, `pointercancel` snaps back; see `SidePanel.md`). Release without closing eases back (`transition-transform`); a closing release leaves `--sheet-drag-offset` on the sheet, which `sheet-out` uses as its `from`, so the exit starts at the finger instead of jumping to rest.
- `AgendaQuickAdd` keeps the sheet mounted while `leaving` (set when `open` turns false while narrow) and unmounts on the sheet's own `animationend`, with a 600ms timeout as fallback so an invisible scrim can never keep blocking the page. While leaving the sheet is `inert`. Reopening during the exit just plays the open animation again.
- Height: the dialog is a bottom-anchored wrapper (`overflow-hidden`, `rounded-t-container`) around a content box that owns the `max-h-[90dvh]` cap (overflow still scrolls inside the form). `lib/dom/use-animated-height.ts` observes the content box (`ResizeObserver`) and, when its height changes, pins the old pixel height on the wrapper, then sets the new one so `transition-[transform,height]` (`--duration-base`, `ease-base`) glides the top edge; the content rides along with that edge (top-pinned), so the footer does not hold still. It hands back to `auto` on `transitionend` (timer fallback). Triggers: kind toggle, the "Herkend" and "Soort zelf gekozen" lines, wrapping text. No animation for the first sizing, while a keyframe (open or exit slide) runs on the wrapper, during a drag (the drag zeroes `transition-duration` inline) or with `prefers-reduced-motion` (`transition-duration` computes to 0): the height then just changes. Chosen over `interpolate-size: allow-keywords` because that is not available in every iOS Safari we serve (unverified here, no device), and the observer works in all of them.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no slide, only a 120ms opacity fade; the height changes at once.
- `SidePanel` has no reusable slide pattern, hence the own keyframes.

## Gotchas
- Quick add: chrono's Dutch locale lacks `20u`, `23-10` and `overmorgen`, hence the own rules. "zondag" typed on a Sunday means next Sunday (chrono's `forwardDate`); `elke zondag` starts today. `volgende week woensdag` counts from the next ISO week (Monday). A slot spanning several all-day days only fills in its first day. The popover always hangs from the header button, also for a slot click.
- `PageHeaderTitle` morphs the title on every previous/next press.
- The ⋯ menu is portalled (`usePopoverPosition`, `strategy: "fixed"`) with `role="menu"`, arrow keys, Escape and focus return; items hidden by a breakpoint are skipped when focusing.
- The sheet and its scrim are `fixed` and portalled to `body`, so no ancestor `filter` matters.
- The mobile header and bottom-sheet shapes follow the viewport, not the container: Ladle stories only baseline the desktop shape. The "Week" button below `lg` hugs its visible face (the hidden face leaves the flow), so it grows when "Toegevoegd" shows. Every view turns into the day view below `lg` (`NARROW_QUERY` in `AgendaCalendar`, 1023px); the `AgendaDayStrip` stories show the strip alone.
- `page.tsx` also needs `20261018000004` (`calendars.color`, the category RPCs): without it the colour read and the settings panel fail.
- `page.tsx` cannot be tried against hosted until the agenda migrations are applied there, `20261018000003` (series, `agenda_items()` with `series_id`) included: without it the timeline function has the old shape and the repeat flag, the panel's rule and the RPCs fail.
- Occurrences are generated in the series' wall-clock zone (`Europe/Amsterdam`), so 09:00 stays 09:00 over the 25 October change. A month or year without the date (31 January, 29 February) is skipped, as in RFC 5545. A series that would hold over 2000 rows is refused (`invalid-input`).
- `getAgendaEvent` and `getMealPlan` are separate reads because `agenda_items` carries no location, description or recipe id.
- A meal whose ingredients were all merged into earlier meals' lines has no basket item of its own, so a second "Week naar mand" would send it again.
