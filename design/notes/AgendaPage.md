## Anatomy
- Header: `PageHeader` with the period as title (never wraps). The title is a button (`PageHeaderTitle` `trigger`, a chevron after the text) that opens `AgendaDatePicker`: a portalled `Dropdown` popover (`role="dialog"`) with an `InputField type="date"` and a **Ga naar datum** button with a trailing `ArrowRight` (`Button` `trailingIcon`); Enter or the button jumps, Escape and an outside press close it and Escape returns focus to the title. It confirms explicitly because a native date field fires `change` for every complete value while typing, so a year typed digit by digit would navigate. `Week 42` (`text-text-secondary`, ISO week of the first shown day) sits beside the title in the week, three-day and list views, from `lg` only (below `lg` it is `max-lg:hidden`: even "20–22 okt" plus the week number pushes + Nieuw onto its own row at 375 px). From `lg`, right-aligned: a `ButtonToggle size="small"` for Dag/Week/Maand/Lijst (`tone="button"`: track in the Filled-button colour, active segment in the + Nieuw colour; `hugFromLg`: hugs its labels, 40 px high like the buttons), then previous / Vandaag / next (Filled), **Plannen** (Filled, `PanelRight`, `aria-pressed` follows the tray), **⋯** (`AgendaMoreMenu`) and **+ Nieuw** (`variant="white"`, so it differs from the Filled buttons). The group wraps under the toggle when the page is too narrow. Below `lg` (tablet portrait counts as mobile): two rows. Row 1: the title left, **⋯** and **+ Nieuw** (`variant="white"`, with text) right. Row 2: the toggle (grows) with previous and next hugging it on the right; Vandaag and the tray toggle move into the ⋯ menu. The groups are `contents` below `lg` and ordered with `max-lg:order-*`, with a zero-height `basis-full` spacer forcing the break. There is no floating + Nieuw button any more. Both + Nieuw buttons open quick add (below).
- Body: `AgendaBody` gives the calendar a definite height (`100dvh` minus the chrome, at least 28rem) and `pr-page-right-equal`; the grid scrolls inside. The calendar fills the width. The `PlanDrawer` is a mode: closed by default, opened by Plannen, remembered in cookie `agenda-tray` (`open` / `closed`, read in `page.tsx` like `agenda-view`, so the first render is right). Open, it sits beside the calendar from `lg` once the page container is at least 44rem wide (container query; the sidebar and the 128px page padding leave a 1024px viewport only about 570px), under it otherwise (opening it then scrolls it into view). Closed, it stays mounted (inert, `visibility: hidden` after the close transition), so it keeps its tab and search. Opening and closing animate (see Plan tray animation).
- Quick add (ADR-0041, `AgendaQuickAdd`, page composition only): **+ Nieuw**, **N** and a click or drag on an empty slot (`onSelectSlot`, which fills in the slot's day and time) open one window; a slot click no longer opens the event panel. From `lg` a portalled popover (`usePopoverPosition`, `strategy: "fixed"`, `role="dialog"`, outside press and Escape close it, focus returns) (`bg-surface-primary`, darker than its `surface-highlight-white` fields, like the sheet) hangs under the header's + Nieuw (`newButtonRef`), whatever opened it. Below `lg` (same `useIsNarrow`) it is a bottom sheet (scrim, grabber, close button, focus trap; focus goes to the dialog itself, `tabIndex={-1}`, so no keyboard opens until an input is tapped; every field on its own row via `stacked`; slides in and out, see Quick-add sheet animation), since `SidePanel` turns into a side drawer from `md`. Content: `ButtonToggle size="small"` Afspraak/Maaltijd/Te doen (the scroll area reserves the scrollbar gutter, so ticking Hele dag does not change the width; automatic until picked; each kind has its icon again: Calendar, Utensils, Columns3; the line "Soort zelf gekozen · terug naar automatisch" only shows when Afspraak was picked by hand and differs from the recognised kind), the text field, a "Herkend" line with the recognised parts in `<mark>` (`bg-event-amber-surface`, it has dark values) and a hint when no day was found ("vandaag", or the slot's day). Rows per kind, every one editable: **Afspraak** title, day + Hele dag, from/to (default one hour), Met (placeholder "Namen", a `CircleHelp` button after the label opens a small popover: "Meerdere namen scheid je met komma's"; Escape or an outside press closes it), Waar (placeholder "Groningen"), Herhalen, Categorie (default or suggested); **Maaltijd** recipe search (`rankRecipes` over the `PlanRecipeList` data, the match preselected), day, moment (default diner); **Te doen** a select of "Nieuwe kaart" or an open card (`PlanCardList` data), title, the target ("Komt in <bord>, kolom <kolom>"), day. Footer: **Meer details** (Afspraak only: opens `AgendaEventPanel` in create mode with `prefill`, people as "Met …" in the description) and **Toevoegen** (Enter). Each edit overrides the parsed value until the text yields a different value for that field (`useOverridable`). Saving calls `createEvent` (with `repeat`), `createMealPlan`, `createPlannedCard` or `planCard`, closes, reloads the window and shows a `Toast`. No time means all-day; "met <namen>" goes to the description; no AI.
- Parser (`lib/agenda/quick-add.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/agenda-quick-add.test.ts`): own rules for times (`20u`, `20.30`, `10:30-12:00`, `om 9`, `'s avonds`), `23-10`, `overmorgen`, `volgende week <dag>`, "hele dag", "met <namen>", "bij <plaats>", repeats (ADR-0042) and the kind; `chrono-node` (`nl` or `en` by UI language, `forwardDate`) reads what is left. Claimed parts are cut out of chrono's input, so names such as Mei or Jan are never months. The reference is the Agenda-zone wall clock built as a local `Date`, and only calendar components are read back. Kind: a task verb wins (people and places then stay in the title), else a recipe or moment word without people or place is a meal, else an event. Category: a word equal to a category name, else the Sociaal/Sport keyword lists when such a category exists, else the default.
- Event panel: one persistent `SidePanel` for edit and for create from quick add's "Meer details" (`prefill`); an imported event opens it read-only (disabled fields, no footer, "Uit <agendanaam>" above them). A saved event, imported or own, also shows "Gekoppeld" with its recipes, cards and notes (`AgendaEventLinks.md`). Delete is the two-step `DeleteConfirm`.
- Categorie (ADR-0040): an own event has a select of the user's own calendars, each with its colour dot (`InputField` `leading`), under the title. Create preselects the default calendar ("Persoonlijk"). An imported event shows no field, only "Uit <agendanaam>".
- Repeat (ADR-0042): an own event has **Herhalen** (`Niet`, `Elke dag`, `Elke week`, `Om de week`, `Elke maand`, `Elk jaar`) and, when it repeats, **Eindigt** (`Nooit` / `Op datum` with a date field), below the end date. An imported event shows neither. Saving or deleting an occurrence of a series (`item.recurring`) swaps the footer for the scope choice, in the stacked `DeleteConfirm` form: a prompt, then `Alleen deze` / `Deze en volgende` / `Alle` and `Annuleren`. `Alleen deze` is left out of the save choice when the rule was changed, since it leaves the rule alone. An occurrence has no `Niet`; a stored rule none of the choices makes shows as `Aangepast`.
- Meal panel (`AgendaMealPanel`, `SidePanel`): recipe (a `Button` that opens the recipe page), moment (select), servings (`PortionStepper`), date (disabled while the meal is attached to an event), "Naar mand" (`AnimatedAddToBasketButton` with the #429 basket popover), Verwijderen (`DeleteConfirm`) and Opslaan. "Naar mand" saves pending edits first so the basket gets the servings on screen.
- Above the calendar: basket notices after a sync deleted an event with a meal (`AgendaSyncNotices`). `page.tsx` also starts the lazy sync in `after()`.
- Day headers start with a capital (`capitalize` in `AgendaCalendar`, first letter only, `toLocaleUpperCase(locale)`): "Ma 19", "Mon 19"; day, week and month views.
- Clicks: own event opens the event panel; meal opens the meal panel; deadline and planned card open the card in Te doen (`/te-doen?card=<id>`).

## Keyboard shortcuts
`useAgendaShortcuts` (`document` `keydown`) maps `lib/agenda/shortcuts.ts`: `T` today, `D`/`W`/`M`/`L` view, `←`/`→` previous/next, `P` plan tray, `N` quick add (ADR-0041). It does nothing while typing (input, textarea, select, contenteditable), with Ctrl, Cmd, Alt or Shift held, for an already handled key (`defaultPrevented`, so the view toggle's own arrow keys win), or while a panel or popover is open: `shortcutsBlocked` is the event panel, meal panel, calendars panel or a header popover (`onOverlayChange` from the header: the ⋯ menu and the date picker). The ⋯ menu's **Sneltoetsen** item (desktop only) shows the list as a menu step. `W` below `lg` stores `week`, which shows as three days. The view toggle follows `view` (`ButtonToggle` `value`), so a key press moves it.

## Deliberate decisions
- **Time zone**: one fixed zone, `Europe/Amsterdam` (`AGENDA_TIME_ZONE`), until Atlas stores one per user. The calendar runs in the browser zone and shows wall-clock strings; `spanToUtc` reads them in the Agenda zone. The now line and "today" in the grid follow the browser zone.
- **View per device**: cookie `agenda-view`, so the first paint has the right view. `threeDay` is never stored.
- **Colour and visibility** (ADR-0040): `getAgendaItems` reads `calendars.color` in one extra query and `lib/agenda/map.ts` puts it on each event (`calendarId` → `color`; unknown id → blue). `page.tsx` also reads cookie `agenda-hidden` (`lib/agenda/hidden.ts`: calendar ids and the kinds `meal`, `planned_card`, `deadline`, dot-separated) and the calendar overview (`getCalendarOverview`); `AgendaPage` filters the loaded items by that set before the calendar gets them, so hidden items stay loaded and reappear at once. A hidden category still takes new events; they just do not show until it is shown again.
- **Changing an event's category** is `updateEvent` with `calendarId`: the fields are written first (with the series scope), then `move_event_calendar` moves the event. On a series occurrence the scope applies: `this` takes the occurrence out of its series (its date joins `excluded_on`) as a single event, `following` moves it and the later occurrences into a new series of the new calendar, `all` moves the whole series. Attached meal plans follow their event.
- **Data**: the server renders the month of today plus a week each side (`loadWindow`); leaving it calls `loadAgendaRange` and keeps the old items until the new ones arrive. After a save, delete or plan the window reloads.
- **Drag and resize save at once**: items update optimistically; a refusal calls `revert` and restores the items.
- **Planned cards**: `cards.planned_for` is a date. Dropping a card from the Kaarten tab, picking a day in its plan menu, or dragging a planned item to another day calls `planCard({ cardId, plannedFor })` (`te-doen/actions.ts`; `plannedFor` is required, `null` clears). A planned item always stays an all-day item: dropped on an hour slot it still takes only the date. Dragging it onto the plan drawer (`onItemDropOut`) sets `null`. Access is the cards policies alone, no app-side ownership check (ADR-0035): a refused write reads `read-only`, a card RLS hides reads `not-found`. After a save the drawer's copy of the card and the window reload. Tapping a planned item opens the card in Te doen.
- **Meals are day items**: always all-day, on their date, grouped per moment (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) and ordered by `position` in the all-day zone (`compareEventOrder`), never a block on the hour grid. A meal attached to an event takes that event's date, not its time.
- **Planning**: dropping a recipe on a day makes a meal plan (`planned_on`, moment `dinner`, the recipe's servings, last in its group). Dropping it on an event (found by `data-agenda-event-id` under the pointer) attaches it (`calendar_event_id`), so it follows the event. Dragging a meal to another day sets `planned_on` and detaches it. The plan buttons (drawer row, recipe page `...` menu) do the same with a chosen day and moment.
- **Week naar mand** lives in the ⋯ menu (a basket choice replaces the items when there is more than one basket; success shows a `Toast`) and as the footer of the Recepten tab (`PlanRecipeList`, with a one-line explanation). Both call the same `onSend`. It sends the visible week (Monday to Monday, from the shown date, also in month view); meals without a basket item (`shopping_list_items.meal_plan_id`) are scaled to their own servings and the same ingredient and unit are summed. A merged line carries the first contributing meal's id (see `docs/open-questions.md#agenda-week-basket-merged-line-attribution`). Nothing to send is an error message (`nothing-to-send`), and the button shows success only after a successful result.
- **Own events only in a manual calendar, synced ones read-only**: `actions.ts` checks both (`read-only`) on top of RLS and maps `42501` to the same code. Errors are codes, never database text (`agenda.errors.*`).
- **Recurring own events are materialised** (ADR-0042, migration `20261018000003`): a `calendar_event_series` row holds the rule, each occurrence is a `calendar_events` row with `series_id`, `occurrence_on` and `series_detached`. The writes are `security invoker` RPCs (`create_event_series`, `update_event_series`, `delete_event_series`, `extend_event_series`); `actions.ts` picks the RPC from `series_id` and maps `AG403/AG404/AG422` to `read-only`, `not-found`, `invalid-input`. `page.tsx` calls `extend_event_series` in `after()` next to the lazy sync, so a series keeps 400 days ahead. Scopes: `this` detaches the row; `following` ends the series the day before and moves this and the later (not detached) rows to a new series, keeping their ids, or changes the series in place on its first occurrence; `all` changes the series and the rows from today on that are not detached, so earlier ones stay history. Rows are paired with the new dates in date order, so a changed rule drops or adds rows at the end. Delete follows the same scopes; one deleted occurrence goes into `excluded_on`. Drag and resize call `moveEvent`, which sets `series_detached`. A dragged occurrence keeps its `occurrence_on`.
- **The timeline's repeat flag** comes from `series_id` in `agenda_items()` (dropped and recreated with the extra column), not a second query: a month of daily series would put hundreds of ids in a `.in()` filter. `AgendaEventItem.recurring` is set only when `series_id` is set, and `getAgendaEvent` returns the series rule (`repeat`) for the panel.
- All-day events are stored as UTC midnights with an exclusive end; the panel shows the last day inclusive.
- `position` is not in the timeline function, so `getAgendaItems` reads it for the meals in one extra query.

## Plan tray animation (#550)
- **Variant shipped: animated width.** Beside the calendar (`lg` + `@min-[44rem]`) `AgendaBody` is a grid: `grid-template-columns` goes `minmax(0,1fr) 0px` -> `minmax(0,1fr) var(--plan-tray-width)` and `column-gap` 0 -> `stack-lg` in one transition, so the calendar shrinks with the tray and its events scale along. The tray wrapper clips (`overflow-hidden`); its content keeps `--plan-tray-width` (20rem) the whole time, so text never reflows, and slides in `--plan-tray-shift` (12px) from the right while fading 0 -> 1.
- **Timing**: open `--plan-tray-open-duration` 280ms, close `--plan-tray-close-duration` 200ms (closing is quicker, since the user already knows what goes away), both on `--plan-tray-ease` = `--blur-fade-ease` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, the platform's entering curve). Variables live next to the blur-fade ones in `app/globals.css`.
- **Why width and not a fallback**: FullCalendar re-lays out on each frame of the width change. Measured in headless Chromium (Ladle `AgendaPageLayout--Navigation`, 1500px wide, week view): the calendar's width moves smoothly over the 280ms; frames stay at about 16ms apart except one 25-34ms frame at the moment of opening (tray mount and the first relayout), none at closing (max 9ms). That is not visibly stuttering, so the fallback (calendar jumps, only the tray animates) was not needed. If a slower device janks, the fallback is to drop the `transition-[grid-template-columns,column-gap]` classes on the grid and keep the tray content's transition.
- **Closed**: the wrapper is `inert` at once and gets `visibility: hidden` after the close duration (visibility transition with a delay), so the closed tray is neither focusable nor in the accessibility tree, and its state is kept (no unmount).
- **Under the calendar** (narrow): no height animation. The wrapper is `hidden` when closed; opening plays `animate-blur-in` (not on first paint, only after the prop changes).
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`**: no column or shift transition; the content only fades, 120ms (`--blur-fade-reduced-duration`), linear.

## Quick-add sheet animation
- Open: the sheet slides `translateY(100%)` -> 0 over `--sheet-open-duration` 320ms, the scrim fades in alongside. Close (X, scrim, Escape, add, Meer details): slides down over `--sheet-close-duration` 220ms, scrim fades out, then it unmounts. Curve `--sheet-ease` = `--blur-fade-ease`. Variables sit next to the plan-tray ones in `app/globals.css`; utilities `animate-sheet-in/out`, `animate-scrim-in/out`.
- `AgendaQuickAdd` keeps the sheet mounted while `leaving` (set when `open` turns false while narrow) and unmounts on the sheet's own `animationend`, with a 600ms timeout as fallback so an invisible scrim can never keep blocking the page. While leaving the sheet is `inert`. Reopening during the exit just plays the open animation again.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no slide, only a 120ms opacity fade.
- `SidePanel` and `PlanDrawer` have no reusable slide pattern (the drawer animates width), hence the own keyframes.

## Token gaps and flags
- `h-[calc(100dvh-…)]`, `min-h-[28rem]`, `lg:@min-[44rem]:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_…]` and `h-[32rem]` in `AgendaBody` (the tray width is `--plan-tray-width`, 20rem) are layout sizes, not scale values. Same for quick add: the popover's `w-104` and `max-h-[calc(100dvh-10rem)]`, the sheet's `max-h-[90dvh]` and the footer's `pb-[max(1rem,env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` (as in `SidePanel`).
- No token for a disabled field: read-only inputs look editable; a notice says why.
- Icons borrowed from the closed set: view toggle `Calendar`, `Columns3`, `Table`, `List`; the recipe link uses `CookingPot`; the Sneltoetsen menu item uses `Lightbulb` (no keyboard icon).

## Gotchas
- Quick add: chrono's Dutch locale lacks `20u`, `23-10` and `overmorgen`, hence the own rules. "zondag" typed on a Sunday means next Sunday (chrono's `forwardDate`); `elke zondag` starts today. `volgende week woensdag` counts from the next ISO week (Monday). A slot spanning several all-day days only fills in its first day. The popover always hangs from the header button, also for a slot click.
- `PageHeaderTitle` morphs the title on every previous/next press.
- The ⋯ menu is portalled (`usePopoverPosition`, `strategy: "fixed"`) with `role="menu"`, arrow keys, Escape and focus return; items hidden by a breakpoint are skipped when focusing.
- The sheet and its scrim are `fixed` and portalled to `body`, so no ancestor `filter` matters.
- The mobile header and bottom-sheet shapes follow the viewport, not the container: Ladle stories only baseline the desktop shape. The "Week" button below `lg` hugs its visible face (the hidden face leaves the flow), so it grows when "Toegevoegd" shows. The week turns into three days below `lg` (`NARROW_QUERY` in `AgendaCalendar`, 1023px).
- `page.tsx` also needs `20261018000004` (`calendars.color`, the category RPCs): without it the colour read and the settings panel fail.
- `page.tsx` cannot be tried against hosted until the agenda migrations are applied there, `20261018000003` (series, `agenda_items()` with `series_id`) included: without it the timeline function has the old shape and the repeat flag, the panel's rule and the RPCs fail.
- Occurrences are generated in the series' wall-clock zone (`Europe/Amsterdam`), so 09:00 stays 09:00 over the 25 October change. A month or year without the date (31 January, 29 February) is skipped, as in RFC 5545. A series that would hold over 2000 rows is refused (`invalid-input`).
- `getAgendaEvent` and `getMealPlan` are separate reads because `agenda_items` carries no location, description or recipe id.
- A meal whose ingredients were all merged into earlier meals' lines has no basket item of its own, so a second "Week naar mand" would send it again.
